New package: share HTTP services with the members of a mesh. Upstream kinds (direct, bridged through a loopback Bridge), the sharing policy, the probe, the service table with a live catalog and pause, invite links with the join blob in the fragment, join outcomes, Guest stamping, WebSockets carried inside one full-duplex mesh request, and the Bridge itself (`./bridge`, Node and Bun) with per-Guest cookie jars.
